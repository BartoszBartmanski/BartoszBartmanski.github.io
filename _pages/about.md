## About

I am a quantitative scientist at EMBL Heidelberg (Zimmermann-Kogadeeva group).
I build the software, pipelines and models that turn biological and
pharmacological data into decisions. My background is a PhD in Applied
Mathematics from Oxford, followed by roles in research software engineering and
computational biology.

My work covers three connected areas:

Models: population PK/PD and PBPK, mechanistic and stochastic systems, ML for model diagnostics  
Pipelines and data: reproducible Snakemake/Nextflow workflows on HPC, multi-omics and imaging data in FAIR formats, LLM-based literature extraction  
Software: tested, documented Python/R packages with CI/CD, including Maphelios on PyPI  

I care about good practice: unit testing, continuous integration and clear
documentation.
